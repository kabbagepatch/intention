About

Growing up, whenever I wanted to use a computer, I'd have to go to the computer room, with a specific purpose in mind, even if it was just to get on Facebook to chat with friends or watch a YouTube video.

Now, the internet has become a constant presence, and turning my PC on is the default first thing I do every morning.

This app aims to bring back a bit more intention to your screen time by simply asking what you plan to do, rather than aimlessly bounce between tabs. Enjoy and don't forget to take breaks!
